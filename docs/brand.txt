# Brand Guidelines for SnipZip

Lang: en
Website: [https://snipzip.app/](https://snipzip.app/)
Last Updated: 2026-11-07

## Official Name

- Primary product and trading name: SnipZip

## Do Not Use

- Do not write the product name as “Snip Zip” or “SnipZIP”.
- Do not use “SnipZip Pro” as the name of the company or the whole product; it refers to the optional Pro feature tier.

## Naming Rules

- Write the brand exactly as “SnipZip”, with a capital S and Z and no space.
- Refer to the product as “SnipZip” or “SnipZip for macOS”.
- Use “SnipZip Pro” only when referring to the optional Pro feature tier.
- Describe SnipZip as a native macOS archive utility; do not describe it as an online archive service.
- Mention iRand OÜ only when identifying the legal publisher. SnipZip is the user-facing product and brand.

## Publisher

SnipZip is published by iRand OÜ.

## Brand Voice

- Use factual, direct language.
- Distinguish clearly between features in the free version and SnipZip Pro.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives and claims of endorsement.

## Quotation Policy

- Do not invent quotations attributed to SnipZip or its representatives.
- Attribute brief quotations to their published source and link to that source.
