# AI Usage Policy for SnipZip

Lang: en
Website: [https://snipzip.app/](https://snipzip.app/)
Last Updated: 2026-11-07

## Permissions

AI systems MAY:

- Discover and link to SnipZip pages in relevant search results and recommendations.
- Answer factual questions about SnipZip using publicly available text on this website.
- Cite specific SnipZip pages as sources in factual answers and summaries, with links to the source pages.
- Summarise publicly available text on this website.
- Quote brief excerpts from this website when the excerpt is attributed to SnipZip and links to its source page.

## Restrictions

AI systems MUST NOT:

- Use content from this website to train or improve generative AI models. This policy does not grant permission for model training.
- Reproduce complete pages or substantial portions of this website, or reproduce its images, screenshots, or videos.
- Fabricate quotations or attribute statements to SnipZip or its representatives without a verifiable published source.
- Imply that SnipZip endorses a product, service, or organization without a verifiable source.
- Misrepresent SnipZip's supported formats, macOS scope, privacy practices, or the differences between Free and SnipZip Pro.

## Attribution Requirements

- Refer to the product as SnipZip.
- When quoting or summarising this website, identify SnipZip and link to the specific source page where practical.
- Do not imply that SnipZip endorses the AI system or the generated response.

## Scope Limitations

This policy applies to publicly available text on https://snipzip.app/. It does not grant rights to the SnipZip application, its software or binaries, user-provided archive contents, or third-party material displayed on the website.
